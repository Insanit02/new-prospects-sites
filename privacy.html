Privacy notice

Last updated October 28, 2026.

This site is run by New Prospects. This page explains in plain language what information we handle and why. It is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.

What this website collects

Nothing on our own pages. We don't use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics, and the contact form on the home page does not send anything to a server: it only opens your email app with your message ready. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub (a third party) may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.

What we receive by email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach. We use it only to reply to you, quote your project, and deliver the work you ask for. We keep project emails for as long as the project and normal business records require, and delete them on request unless the law requires us to keep them.

Files you send for data or spreadsheet jobs

We use your files only for your job. We don't sell, share or publish them. Files are deleted within 14 days after delivery, or sooner on request. Please don't send passwords, card numbers, government ID numbers or health records.

Payments

Payments are made through Stripe payment links. Stripe handles your card details. We never see or store them. We receive confirmation that a payment was made.

Cold emails we send

When we contact a business by email, we use an address the business has published for customer contact. Every such message includes our mailing address and a way to opt out. Reply "unsubscribe" and we won't contact you again.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email newestprospects@gmail.com.
