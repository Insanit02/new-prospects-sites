Terms of service

Last updated October 28, 2026. These are plain-language terms for our standard jobs, not legal advice. Anything specific to your project is written in the quote we send you, and the quote controls if it differs.

1. Who we are

New Prospects ("we") provides small websites, website quick fixes, and data and spreadsheet services. Contact: newestprospects@gmail.com.

2. Quotes and scope

Every job starts with a written scope, price and timeline sent by email. Work outside that scope is quoted separately before we do it. Package contents are listed on our pricing section.

3. Prices and payment

  • Website packages (Starter $149, Professional $249, Premium $399): 50% deposit to start. The other 50% is due after you approve the preview and before launch.
  • Quick Fix ($49): paid after the fix is delivered.
  • Data and spreadsheet jobs: fixed price quoted after we see your file. We may ask for a deposit on larger jobs, stated in the quote.
  • Maintenance (optional): $29 per month, cancel anytime by email.
  • Payments are made by secure payment link through Stripe. Domain names, paid hosting, and third-party subscriptions are billed to you by those providers.

4. Timelines

Delivery times (for example 2 business days for Starter) start when we have the deposit and the content and access we need from you. Delays in getting these from you extend the timeline.

5. Revisions and preview approval

Each package includes the number of revision rounds shown on the pricing section. We send a preview before you pay the balance. You pay the balance when you approve it.

6. Warranty

For 14 days after launch, or after delivery of a Quick Fix or data job, we fix defects in our own work at no charge. This doesn't cover changes you or others make afterwards, or problems with third-party services.

7. Cancellation and refunds

Either of us may cancel in writing. You pay for work already done up to that point. If you have paid a deposit that is more than the work completed, we refund the difference.

8. Ownership

When the full price is paid, you own the final site files and deliverables. We may show a finished website in our portfolio unless you tell us in writing you'd rather we didn't.

9. Your content and access

You confirm you own or have the right to use the text, photos and logos you give us. We ask for collaborator or contributor invites, not passwords. Please don't send passwords. If you do, we will delete them and ask you to change them.

10. What we don't promise

We don't guarantee search rankings, website traffic, leads or sales. We aren't responsible for outages at third-party services such as hosting providers and registrars.

11. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability for any job is limited to the fees you paid us for that job.

12. Sample work

Sites on this page for fictional businesses (Greenline Yard Co., BrightNest Cleaning, Apex Shine Detailing) are samples made by us, not client projects. The "before" examples and sample spreadsheet data are illustrations, not real client files.

13. Changes

We may update these terms. The terms in effect when we send you a quote apply to that job.
