Frequently asked questions

Are addresses uploaded?

No. Pasted text and CSV files are read in this browser. They are not uploaded to New Prospects. Closing the tab discards them. Checking a license key sends the key to Gumroad, not the addresses.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Free cleans, flags, and de-duplicates up to 25 addresses, and shows an on-screen preview of the first label sheet. Pro is $5, one time: unlimited addresses, printable Avery 5160 / 8160 and Avery 5163 sheets, a return-address sheet, and CSV or Excel export. The list still stays in the browser.

Which files can I start from?

A CSV export from Google Contacts, Outlook, Apple Contacts, or Excel, or rows pasted from a spreadsheet. If the columns are not recognized, you can map them. You can also paste each address as a block of 2 to 4 lines with a blank line between addresses.

What does cleaning change?

Extra spaces and repeated commas are removed. All-caps and all-lowercase words are changed to usual capitalization, while forms such as PO Box, NE, SW, Apt, and III stay in their usual form. Full US state names become two-letter codes. ZIP codes are written as 5 digits or ZIP+4, and a leading zero that Excel dropped is put back (2134 becomes 02134).

How are duplicates and households handled?

Rows with the same street and ZIP, after a simple normalization such as Street versus St, are offered as one label. You can name it from the shared last name (Jane & John Smith), use The Smith Family, or keep the first name line. Each merge has an undo. A likely non-US address is kept and marked.

How do I print on Avery labels?

Pro opens the browser print dialog, where you can print or save as PDF. Use US Letter, scale 100 percent (not fit to page), margins none, and headers and footers off. Avery 5160 and 8160 are 30 labels per sheet, 1 inch by 2-5/8 inch. Avery 5163 is 10 labels per sheet, 2 inch by 4 inch. Print one page on plain paper and hold it up to a label sheet before printing on labels.

Where is the license key checked?

Your browser asks Gumroad's public license API. New Prospects does not receive the key. A saved key stays in local storage on this device.
