Frequently asked questions

Is this a guarantee?

No. It is an aid. A low score does not mean a message is safe, and a high score does not prove a crime. It looks for common patterns. It does not contact a bank, the sender, or a scam database.

Is the message uploaded?

No. The text stays in this browser. Closing the tab discards it.

What does the score mean?

Each matching pattern adds points, up to 100. Zero is no common red flags. Under 40 is some red flags. 40 or more is a high risk pattern. The points are a rule total, not a probability.

What should I do if it looks like a scam?

Do not send money, codes, or passwords, and do not open links from the message. Look up the company yourself. You can report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov or ic3.gov.

Will it catch every scam?

No. New wording gets through. Use it as a second look, not as permission to trust a message.
