Frequently asked questions

Is my bank statement uploaded?

No. The CSV or PDF is read in your browser. It is not uploaded to New Prospects or anyone else. Closing the tab discards it.

What does the free version include?

The first 50 transactions in the file, in the order they appear, plus the on-screen summary, chart, and repeating-charge list for those rows.

What does Pro include?

Pro is $9, one time. It removes the 50-row limit, saves custom categories in this browser, and exports CSV or Excel. The file still never leaves the browser.

How are categories chosen?

By keywords you can edit, such as groceries, dining, gas, subscriptions, utilities, rent, transfers, income, and shopping. A longer keyword wins. This is a guess, not a bank's category and not financial advice.

Will it understand every bank file?

It looks for a header like Date, Description, and Amount, or Debit and Credit. Text-based PDFs use the same table guess as the free PDF-to-Excel tool. Scans have no text to read. Dates written month/day/year are read as US dates.

Where is the license key checked?

Your browser asks Gumroad's public license API. New Prospects does not receive the key. The result is saved in local storage on this device.
