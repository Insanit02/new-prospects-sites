Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you email us.

Who is behind New Prospects?

New Prospects is a small independent business. You deal with the business by email and in writing, and we don't publish personal names. Your agreement is with New Prospects, and every price, scope and delivery date is written down before you pay.

Do you have reviews or past clients?

Not yet. We started in October 2026 and won't make up testimonials. That's why the samples are clearly labeled, why you see a preview before paying the balance, and why Quick Fixes are paid after delivery.

What does the free preview mean?

Ask and we'll send a one-screen mock-up showing how we'd improve your page, at no cost and no obligation. For a full site, you approve a preview before you pay the remaining 50%.

Do you need my password?

No. For Wix, Squarespace, WordPress and similar sites, we use a collaborator or contributor invite. For anything else we send you the exact code to paste. Please never send passwords by email.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once the balance is paid, you own the final site files. Hosting and your domain live in accounts under your name.

What does it cost to keep the site online?

Free hosting is available (GitHub Pages or Netlify). A custom domain is about $12 to $20 a year, paid to the registrar. Our optional maintenance plan is $29 a month, cancel anytime.

How do I pay?

By secure payment link through Stripe. We never see or handle your card details.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us what's wrong during the revision rounds and we'll fix it. You haven't paid the balance until you approve the preview. See the terms for cancellations and refunds of unused deposits.

What happens to files I send for data or spreadsheet work?

We use them only for your job and delete them on request after delivery. See the privacy notice.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise that. We set up the on-page basics properly (titles, descriptions, schema, mobile speed) and give you a checklist for your Google Business Profile.
