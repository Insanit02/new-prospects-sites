Free PDF to Excel, in your browser

Turn a text-based PDF into a CSV or Excel file on this page. Your file is not uploaded. This is a best-effort split for simple tables. It does not check totals. Scanned pages and complicated layouts need the paid service.

Your PDF never leaves this browser. It is not uploaded to New Prospects or to anyone else. We do not see it. PDF.js reads the text and SheetJS writes the spreadsheet, and both of those files are part of this website. Closing the tab discards the PDF. Clear does the same while you stay on the page.

What this free tool can and cannot do

  • Text you can select. If a PDF reader will not let you highlight the words, this tool cannot see them. That includes scans and photos of pages.
  • Rows are lines of text, grouped by how high they sit on the page. Columns are gaps that line up down the page. Wrapped sentences, merged cells and tables without a clear gap often land in the wrong cell.
  • Numbers and dates stay as text. Nothing is compared with the totals printed on the PDF.
  • Sideways text is skipped. Some unusual fonts come out blank or garbled.
  • One PDF at a time, up to 25 pages and 15 MB. A longer file, a scan, or a table that has to be right belongs with the paid conversion.
